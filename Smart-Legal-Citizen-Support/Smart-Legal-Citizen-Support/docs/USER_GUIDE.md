# Citizen user guide

1. Start from the home page and search legal information or browse complaint guides and citizen services.
2. Open an article to read its jurisdiction, review date, and available source link. Verify current rules with the linked official source.
3. In an article, log in to save or remove it from your personal library.
4. On a complaint guide, review the general preparation steps and tap checklist boxes. Sign in to save progress. The website does not file the complaint; continue on the linked official portal.
5. Search the advocate directory by name, practice area, or city. A `Verified` label means only that an administrator set this platform status; independently confirm qualifications. Fictional demo profiles are labelled.
6. Ask a general question in the assistant. It searches the site's reviewed/published library and returns matches where available. It is not generative AI, legal advice, or a crisis service.
7. Use the dashboard to manage saved content. Profile lets you change permitted details and password. Account deletion removes your user and saved items; feedback remains without your account association.
8. Use feedback for site issues. Do not submit confidential case facts, identity documents, passwords, OTPs, or evidence.

## Emergency
For immediate danger, do not wait for CivicLaw. The emergency page lists India ERSS 112 and links to its official source. Verify current regional instructions directly with the service.

## Administrator
Create a superuser using `python manage.py createsuperuser`, log in at `/admin/`, and manage categories, articles, advocate records/status, guides, services, and feedback. Review and source-check content before publication. Demo advocate records are fictional.
