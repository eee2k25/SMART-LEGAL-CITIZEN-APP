URGENT SUPPORT

Emergency information

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services directly. Do not wait for a website or assistant.

India: Emergency Response Support System — 112

Use 112 for emergency response. Confirm current regional arrangements through the Government of India’s official ERSS information.

Visit official ERSS website ↗

For legal support

For urgent legal matters, contact a qualified lawyer or the relevant Legal Services Authority. This website cannot dispatch help or file a complaint.

Emergency reference: Government of India ERSS, 112.gov.in. Details may change; verify with official sources.
