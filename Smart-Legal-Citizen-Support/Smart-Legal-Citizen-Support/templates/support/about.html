ABOUT THE PROJECT

Practical guidance for everyday questions

Smart Legal & Citizen Support Platform is a web application concept that brings general legal information, complaint preparation and citizen-service discovery together.

What this platform does

It offers searchable content, a curated-library assistant, an advocate directory, complaint checklists, public-service links and account tools.

What it does not do

It does not provide individualized legal advice, file complaints, contact advocates, verify official case status, or replace public authorities. Content requires ongoing review by qualified people.
