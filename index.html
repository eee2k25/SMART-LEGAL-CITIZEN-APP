CivicLawAcademic project
SMART LEGAL & CITIZEN SUPPORT

Legal and public-service guidance, made clearer.

A responsive Django web application for exploring general legal information, searching an advocate directory, preparing complaint checklists, and discovering citizen services.

Open live application → View source on GitHub

What you can explore

Legal information

Search general information with jurisdiction and source references.

Advocate directory

Try name, practice-area, and location filters using labelled demo profiles.

Complaint preparation

Review general steps and save checklist progress with an account.

Citizen services

Browse service information and continue to official websites.

Information assistant

Searches the platform library and points to matching articles; it is not generative AI.

Built with

Python, Django, Django Templates, SQLite locally, and PostgreSQL support for deployment.

Scope note: This is an academic demonstration, not a law firm or government service. Demo advocate profiles are fictional; the platform does not file complaints or contact lawyers. Legal content is general information, not legal advice.
